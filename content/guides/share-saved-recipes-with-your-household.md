# How do I share saved recipes with my partner or household?

Short answer: stop sending each other videos and keep one shared place for recipes instead. That can be a shared note, a shared Reminders list for groceries, or a recipe app with household sharing, where everyone sees the same recipes, the same plan for the week, and the same grocery list. The point is one copy that everyone edits, not a group chat full of links.

We make Flav, which has household sharing. The free ways come first.

## Why sending links doesn't work

Most couples and housemates share recipes by DM: "we should make this". It feels like sharing, but it isn't. The link lands in a chat, the chat moves on, and three weeks later nobody can find it. When one of you cooks it and changes the amounts, the other doesn't know. And whoever does the shopping is working from a screenshot.

What you want is a shared recipe box, not a shared inbox.

## The free way: Notes and Reminders

On iPhone you can do this with what you already have.

1. **A shared note for recipes.** Make a note called "Recipes we cook". Tap the share button and invite the other person so you can both edit it. Paste each recipe in, ingredients and steps, with the video link.
2. **A shared Reminders list for groceries.** Make a Groceries list in Reminders and share it the same way. Both of you add items, and whoever's at the store ticks them off.
3. **A simple plan.** At the top of the note, write the three or four things you're cooking this week.

It works. The weak spot is the note. After twenty recipes it's one long scroll, and copying captions in by hand gets old.

## Things to agree on first

Whatever you use, a few small rules save arguments:

- **One place.** If a recipe isn't in the shared box, it doesn't count. No "I sent it to you on Instagram".
- **Whoever cooks it fixes it.** If the amounts were off, the person who cooked updates the recipe that night.
- **The list is the list.** If it's not on the grocery list, it's not getting bought.
- **A short "this week".** Pick a few meals, not seven. Plans that are too full get ignored by Wednesday.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the easiest way to share recipes with my partner?**
A: Keep them in one shared place you both edit, rather than sending links. A shared note in Notes works for free. A recipe app with household sharing adds a shared meal plan and grocery list.

**Q: Can we share a grocery list between two iPhones?**
A: Yes. In Reminders, open the list, tap the share button and invite the other person. In Flav, the grocery list is shared with everyone in your household.

**Q: Are my saved recipes visible to my household in Flav?**
A: Not unless you share them. Imports are private by default. Recipes you share with your household, along with the shared plan and grocery list, are visible to its members.

**Q: Can ChatGPT plan meals for two people?**
A: It can suggest a week of meals and a grocery list if you tell it what you both like. It can't see your saved recipes or keep a shared list, so you'd still copy the result somewhere you both use.
