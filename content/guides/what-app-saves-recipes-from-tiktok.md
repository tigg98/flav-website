# What app saves recipes from TikTok?

Short answer: ReciMe, Pestle, Paprika, Honeydew, Mela and Flav all say they import recipes from TikTok links. Samsung Food saves recipes from websites but doesn't list TikTok. TikTok's own Favorites saves the video, not the recipe. We make Flav, so weigh what we say about it accordingly.

## The four things that matter

1. **What it reads.** Most apps read the TikTok's caption and page. When the creator only says the recipe out loud, an app has to work from the spoken words, and few say clearly whether they do.
2. **What it does after.** A grocery list, a cooking view with timers, a plan for the week.
3. **Who it's for.** If two people cook, check for household sharing, not just sending a recipe by text.
4. **Cost and platform.** Free limits vary from a few imports to a cap on total recipes. Some are iPhone only.

## TikTok Favorites

Free, one tap, and you can sort saves into collections. It isn't a recipe box: no ingredient list, no grocery list, and if the creator deletes the video, it's gone. [More on that here](/guides/why-tiktok-favorites-is-a-bad-recipe-box).

## ReciMe

The widest list of sources here, and it runs on Android and the web as well as iPhone. Grocery lists sort by aisle, and there's a weekly planner. The free version is limited to 5 imports a week, which is generous if you save a few a week. Its pages don't say how it handles a video with no recipe in the caption, and prices are shown in the app, not on the site.

## Pestle

An indie app with a strong cooking side: hands-free guided cooking with several timers, and an Apple Watch app. When it can't pull a recipe out, it saves a bookmark to the source instead, which we like. The free plan stops at 15 recipes.

## Paprika 4

The long-standing recipe manager, now on a subscription. It runs nearly everywhere, including Android and Windows, with a deep meal planner and pantry tools. Social import is on Premium. If you keep a big collection of website recipes, Paprika is built for that.

## Honeydew

Built around capture: Reels, TikToks, screenshots, cookbook photos. Its site shows it working from a video's transcript, which matters for spoken-only recipes. Social import is on Honeydew Plus.

## Mela

A tidy, pay-once app for iPhone and iPad. It reads the description on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram videos, not the video itself, and uses Reminders for groceries. Its last update was in 2025.

## Samsung Food

Strong on meal planning, shared grocery lists and delivery. Its store pages cover saving from websites and don't mention TikTok, so it's not the pick if your recipes mostly come from short videos.

## Flav

Ours. Share a TikTok, Reel or web page to Flav and it saves a recipe with quantities and ordered steps, private by default. Cook Mode goes step by step with timers, on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch. Recipes go to a meal plan or grocery list, shared with your household if you like.

What it isn't: it's iPhone only, and Flav 2.0 reads the caption and page, not the video. If the creator only says the recipe out loud, you'll get a thin or empty recipe. Reading the video is planned for a later version.

## How to pick

- Recipes mostly in captions, iPhone: any of them works. Pick on price and feel.
- Lots of spoken-only recipes: test each app's free plan with your own examples first.
- Android in the house: ReciMe, Paprika, Honeydew or Samsung Food.
- You want to pay once: Mela, or the lifetime options on Pestle and Flav.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the best app to save recipes from TikTok?**
A: It depends on what you need after saving. ReciMe, Pestle, Paprika, Honeydew, Mela and Flav all import from TikTok links. Compare their free limits, whether they handle recipes that are only spoken, household sharing, and whether you need Android.

**Q: Is there a free app that saves TikTok recipes?**
A: Several have free plans. ReciMe allows 5 imports a week, Flav 10 imports a month, Pestle up to 15 recipes and Paprika up to 50. TikTok Favorites is free but saves only the video.

**Q: Can ChatGPT save recipes from TikTok?**
A: ChatGPT can tidy a caption you paste into it into an ingredient list and steps. It doesn't keep a cookbook, a grocery list or a cooking timer for you, which is what a recipe app adds.

**Q: Which TikTok recipe apps work on Android?**
A: As of November 2026, ReciMe, Paprika, Honeydew and Samsung Food list Android. Flav, Pestle and Mela list only Apple devices.
