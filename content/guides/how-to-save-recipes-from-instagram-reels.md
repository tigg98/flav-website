# How do I save recipes from Instagram Reels?

Short answer: the bookmark on a Reel saves the Reel, not the recipe. To cook from it later, get the ingredients and steps out of the caption and into a note or a recipe app, with the Reel's link kept alongside. Food Reels usually have longer captions than TikToks, so this tends to work well. We make Flav, a recipe app that does this, and we'll show the by-hand way too.

## What Instagram's Save does

Tap the bookmark under a Reel and it goes to your Saved list. Press and hold the bookmark and you can add it to a collection, like "Dinners". Instagram says these collections are private, and they're a decent way to sort videos.

But a saved Reel is still a video. The recipe is in the caption, often below a long intro, and to cook you have to open Instagram, find the collection, open the Reel, expand the caption, and scroll. With wet hands. Every time.

## The free way: copy the caption

1. Open the Reel and tap the caption to expand it.
2. Instagram doesn't always let you select caption text. If it won't, take a screenshot, open it in Photos, and use Live Text to copy the words.
3. Paste the text into a note.
4. Tap the share icon on the Reel and Copy link. Paste the link at the top of the note.
5. Tidy it: ingredients in a list, steps numbered.

For one or two recipes, that's fine. For a habit of saving a few a week, the tidying is where it falls apart.

## The quicker way: share the Reel to a recipe app

In Flav:

1. On the Reel, tap the share icon (the paper plane).
2. Scroll the row at the bottom and tap Share to, or the iPhone share option, to open the share sheet. Instagram's layout moves around between versions, so look for the option that opens the system share sheet.
3. Pick Flav. If it isn't in the row, tap More and add it.
4. Flav reads the caption and the page and saves a recipe: title, ingredients with quantities, steps in order, and the Reel's link.

Or copy the Reel's link, open Flav, and paste it.

Usually that takes a few seconds. A long caption can take about 15. The recipe goes into your own cookbook and stays private.

## When the recipe says "link in bio"

Plenty of food creators on Instagram post a short caption and keep the full recipe on their site. Tap their name, then the link in their bio, and find the recipe there. A blog recipe page is easy to save, because it's already written with amounts and steps. You can share that page to Flav the same way.

If the creator only talks through the recipe in the Reel and doesn't write it anywhere, a caption reader can't help. Flav 2.0 reads the caption and the page, not the video, so a Reel like that will come back thin or empty. [Here's what to do instead](/guides/tiktok-recipe-not-in-caption). The advice is the same for Reels as for TikTok.

## Carousels and posts

Not every Instagram recipe is a Reel. Some are photo carousels with the recipe written on the slides. Those slides are images, not caption text, so a caption reader won't see what's on them. Screenshot the slides and copy the text with Live Text, or check whether the caption repeats it.

## Keep the Reel saved too

There's no harm in saving the Reel in Instagram as well. The video is useful for the one step you can't picture from text. Just don't rely on it as your recipe box. Reels get deleted, accounts go private, and your Saved list will grow faster than you cook.

## FAQ

**Q: Does saving a Reel on Instagram save the recipe?**
A: No. It saves the Reel to your Saved list or a collection. The recipe stays in the caption, and you have to open the Reel and scroll to it each time you cook.

**Q: Are my saved Instagram collections private?**
A: Instagram says saved collections are private and only you can see them. If you save the recipe to a recipe app, check that app's settings too. Flav imports are private unless you share them with your household.

**Q: Can I save a recipe from an Instagram carousel post?**
A: If the recipe is in the caption, yes, the same way as a Reel. If it's written on the slides, those are images. Screenshot them and copy the text with Live Text in Photos.

**Q: Can ChatGPT save recipes from Instagram?**
A: It can clean up a caption you paste into it, turning it into a list and numbered steps. It doesn't keep a cookbook for you, so you'd still need to put the result somewhere you'll find it when you cook.
